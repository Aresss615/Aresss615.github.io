J&J Grocery POS

Checkout, inventory and books for my family’s grocery: barcode scanning, BIR-format receipts with 12% VAT, cash and GCash, and a double-entry journal behind every sale.

My role
Solo developer, rebuilding the store’s existing system
Timeline
2026
Stack
PHP 8 · MySQL · JavaScript · PHPUnit
Status
In testing before rollout

01 / The problem

A checkout that has to answer to the BIR.

I’ve kept the tech running at my family’s grocery since 2022, and the store already had a POS. This is a full rebuild of it.

A grocery checkout in the Philippines does more than take payment. Receipts must follow BIR format, VAT has to be right on every line, customers pay in cash or GCash, and the family needs books that actually balance across branches.

02 / Architecture

Every sale lands in the books.

The terminal runs in a browser with a barcode scanner. A PHP 8 backend handles checkout, inventory and receipts, and posts a journal entry for every sale. MySQL stores products, stock, sales and the journal entries.

  1. Front Checkout terminal
    • Browser screen
    • Barcode scanner
    • Cash and GCash tender
  2. Server PHP 8 app
    • Checkout and inventory
    • BIR-format receipts with VAT
    • Journal posting per sale
  3. Data MySQL
    • Products and stock
    • Sales and receipts
    • Journal entries

03 / Hard problems

What took the most thought.

  1. 01

    VAT that is already in the price

    Shelf prices in the store already include VAT, so the system can’t add 12% on top. It pulls the tax back out of each price instead:

    VAT = price × 12 ÷ 112
    ₱112.00 → VAT ₱12.00, net ₱100.00
  2. 02

    A journal entry behind every sale

    Each sale posts a balanced double-entry journal entry, so every peso the terminal takes in has a matching entry in the books.

  3. 03

    Receipts the BIR will accept

    Every sale prints a receipt in BIR format with the VAT broken out, whether the customer paid in cash or GCash.

04 / Status

In testing before rollout.

The rebuild is covered by PHPUnit tests and is in testing before it replaces the store’s current system.
